Two groups or three? 兩組，還是三組？Week 7 · from the t-test to one-way ANOVA · 從 t 檢定到單因子變異數分析
stage 0 · from the t-test · 從 t 檢定開始

You know two groups. What changes with three?

兩組你已經會了。變成三組，改變了什麼？
Summary 總結
  1. Two levels → t-test. Three or more levels → one-way ANOVA.兩個水準 → t 檢定。三個以上水準 → 單因子變異數分析。
  2. In the design, only the number of levels changes: same factor, same DV, same design. In the test, one assumption changes too (below).在研究設計上，只有水準的數目改變：因子、依變項、設計都一樣。在檢定上，還多改了一個假設（見下）。
  3. One test first. H₀: all population means are equal. H₁: not all equal, at least one pair differs. Then post hoc comparisons for the pairs.先做一個檢定。H₀：所有母體平均數相同。H₁：不是全部相同，至少有一對不同。再用事後比較看配對。

Add a third warm-up加入第三種暖身

Two groups: H₀: μdynamic = μstatic. Three groups: H₀: μdynamic = μjog = μstatic · H₁: not all equal, at least one pair differs.兩組：H₀ 兩個母體平均數相同。三組：H₀ 三個母體平均數全部相同 · H₁：不是全部相同，至少有一對不同。

H₁ does not say that all three differ. The overall question comes first; which pairs differ comes later.H₁ 不是「三組全部互相不同」。先問整體問題，哪兩組不同之後再比較。

Why not three t-tests?為什麼不做三次 t 檢定？

1,000 simulated studies. In each, three groups of 6 athletes are drawn from the same normal population, so we know the three population means are equal (H₀ true). Each study runs three uncorrected Welch t-tests and one Fisher ANOVA.模擬 1,000 個研究。每個研究都從同一個常態母體抽出三組、每組 6 人，所以預先知道三個母體平均數相同（H₀ 成立）。每個研究做三次未校正的 Welch t 檢定，以及一次 Fisher 變異數分析。
Three Welch t-tests: at least one p < .05三次 Welch t：至少一次 p < .05

One Fisher ANOVA of all three: p < .05一次同時比較三組的 Fisher 變異數分析：p < .05

Dashed line = .05. Out of 1,000 studies, not 3,000 tests: a study with two significant comparisons counts once. We know these are false alarms only because the simulation has no real difference; a real study’s p cannot tell. 10.1% is this run, not a fixed rate, and not 5% × 3.虛線 = .05。分母是 1,000 個研究，不是 3,000 次檢定：同一個研究即使有兩個比較顯著，也只算一次。我們知道這些是誤報，只因為模擬一開始就沒有真正的差異；真實研究單靠 p 值分不出來。10.1% 是這一輪的結果，不是固定的錯誤率，也不是 5% × 3。

stage 1 · factor, level or DV? · 因子、水準還是依變項？

Six studies: find the factor, the levels and the DV

六個研究：找出因子、水準和依變項
Summary 總結

Three checks before any tool 選工具前的三個檢查

  1. What makes the groups? → the factor; count its levels.什麼把人分組？→ 因子；數它的水準。
  2. What is measured on everyone? → the DV: a number or a category.每個人身上量什麼？→ 依變項：數值或類別。
  3. Different people or the same people in each level?每個水準是不同的人，還是同一群人？

Watch for 注意

  • A grouping nobody assigned (position, sex) is still a factor.沒有人分派的分組（位置、性別）仍然是因子。
  • Several outcomes are several DVs, not levels.好幾個結果是好幾個依變項，不是水準。
  • A yes / no outcome is a category DV.有／沒有的結果是類別依變項。

The tool follows the design. Change one feature, and the tool can change.工具跟著設計走。改一個特徵，工具就可能改變。

stage 3 · read the result · 讀結果

F(2, 15) = 5.38, p = .017, η² = .42: what does it say?

F(2, 15) = 5.38、p = .017、η² = .42：它說了什麼？
Summary 總結
  • F looks at how different the group means are, compared with how much people vary within the same group; with the two df it gives the p-value.F 看各組平均數之間的差異，相對於同組內人與人之間的波動有多突出；再配合兩個自由度算出 p 值。
  • A significant F: at least one population mean differs. Which pairs: post hoc comparisons (Tukey).F 顯著：至少有一個母體平均數不同。哪些配對：事後比較（Tukey）。
  • η² is an effect size: the share of the total variation in the sample that lies between the groups (.01 small · .06 medium · .14 large). F grows with N; η² does not.η² 是效果量：樣本總變異中，有多少比例屬於組間變異（.01 小 · .06 中 · .14 大）。F 會隨 N 變大；η² 不會。

How to read it 怎麼讀

F = 5.38The differences between the means are 5.38 times what the spread inside the groups would give.平均數之間的差異是組內散布所能造成的 5.38 倍。
(2, 15)Two df: k − 1 = 2 and N − k = 15.兩個自由度：k − 1 = 2 與 N − k = 15。
p = .017If the three population means were equal, an F this large would be rare. It is not the chance that H₀ is true.如果三個母體平均數相同，這麼大的 F 很少見。它不是 H₀ 為真的機率。
Large?F > 1 is not the same as significant. Compare F with what H₀ gives: about 1. With df (2, 15) only 5% of F values pass 3.68, so 5.38 is rare. The cut-off moves with the df (df 1, 10: 4.96 · df 2, 87: 3.10). How large the effect is: η², not F.F > 1 不等於顯著。和 H₀ 下的 F 比：大約是 1。自由度 (2, 15) 時只有 5% 的 F 超過 3.68，所以 5.38 少見。門檻會隨自由度改變（1 與 10：4.96 · 2 與 87：3.10）。效果有多大看 η²，不是看 F。
η² = .4242% of the total variation in jump height lies between the warm-up groups. Not: jumps were 42% higher; not: 42% of athletes benefited.跳躍高度的總變異有 42% 屬於暖身組間。不是跳高了 42%，也不是 42% 的人有效。

Report 報告

F(df₁, df₂) = …, p = …, η² = … ; then each pair: M, SD, difference, 95% CI, p.先寫整體 F、p、η²；再寫每一對：M、SD、差值、95% CI、p。

Before you trust F 相信 F 之前

Independent groups 獨立組From the design: one athlete, one group. No test checks it.來自研究設計：一人一組。沒有檢定能檢查它。
Normal residuals 殘差常態Each athlete minus their own group mean: look at the Q–Q plot; Shapiro–Wilk is a helper (p ≈ .401).每位運動員減去自己那組的平均數：看 Q–Q 圖；Shapiro–Wilk 只是輔助（p ≈ .401）。
Equal population variances 母體變異數相同SDs 2.81 · 2.67 · 2.49, n 6 · 6 · 6, Levene p = .967 (Jamovi). No evidence against equal variances, so Fisher + Tukey today; not proof that they are equal.SD 2.81 · 2.67 · 2.49，n 6 · 6 · 6，Levene p = .967（Jamovi）。沒有證據反對變異數相同，所以今天用 Fisher + Tukey；但不代表證明了變異數相同。
Judgement, not switches 是判斷，不是開關ANOVA does not need every check p > .05, and Levene alone does not choose Fisher or Welch.不是每個檢查都要 p > .05 才能做變異數分析；也不能只靠 Levene 自動決定 Fisher 或 Welch。

The same numbers in Jamovi and in R

在 Jamovi 和 R 得到同樣的數字
Summary 總結
  1. The same four steps as always: name the factor, its levels and the DV · different or the same people · choose the tool · run and read.和平常一樣的四個步驟：說出因子、水準、依變項 · 不同的人還是同一群人 · 選工具 · 執行並讀結果。
  2. Jamovi: Dependent Variable = the DV; Fixed Factors = the factor. R: Jump_cm ~ WarmUp, the DV left of ~, the factor right of ~.Jamovi：Dependent Variable = 依變項；Fixed Factors = 因子。R：Jump_cm ~ WarmUp，~ 左邊是依變項，右邊是因子。
  3. Read the WarmUp row (not Residuals), and ptukey for the pairs.讀 WarmUp 那一列（不是 Residuals），配對看 ptukey。

Watch for 注意

  • Today’s route: ANOVA → ANOVA, Fisher’s F with η² and Tukey. One-Way ANOVA gives Welch’s F by default (F 5.01, df 2 and 9.98); tick Fisher there and you get the same 5.38.今天的路線：ANOVA → ANOVA，Fisher F 加 η² 和 Tukey。One-Way ANOVA 預設是 Welch F（F 5.01，自由度 2 與 9.98）；在那裡勾 Fisher 也會得到同樣的 5.38。
  • Equal variances: Jamovi reports Levene’s test; R’s check_homogeneity() reports Bartlett’s test. Two different tests of the same assumption.變異數相同：Jamovi 報 Levene 檢定；R 的 check_homogeneity() 報 Bartlett 檢定。是兩個不同的檢定，檢查同一個假設。
  • R and Jamovi both print Dynamic − Static = 5.00: the first group minus the second. Write the direction in words.R 和 Jamovi 都印 Dynamic − Static = 5.00：前者減後者。方向用文字寫。
  • R, first time only: run the install.packages line once; then library() every time you open R.R 第一次才要執行 install.packages；之後每次打開 R 都要 library()。
  • summary(fit): fit alone prints only the sums of squares. pw prints its own table, so it needs no summary(); confint(pw) adds the CIs.summary(fit)：只打 fit 只會印出平方和。pw 本身就會印出表，不用 summary()；confint(pw) 加上信賴區間。
  • report(fit) writes a sentence for you. It does not say which pairs differ and it is not APA (p = 0.017, Eta2): check it, do not copy it.report(fit) 會自動寫一句話：它沒說哪幾對不同，也不是 APA 格式（p = 0.017、Eta2）；要檢查，不能照抄。

R 
install.packages(c("psych", "effectsize", "report",
                   "emmeans", "performance", "see"))   # once
df <- read.csv("Your location/W7_WarmUp_Demo_Data_2026.csv")

# 0) Descriptives by group
library(psych)
describeBy(df$Jump_cm, group = df$WarmUp)

# 1) Ensure factor
df$WarmUp <- factor(df$WarmUp)

# 2) ANOVA + Effect sizes
# left of ~: the DV (Jump_cm 依變項) · right of ~: the factor (WarmUp 因子)
fit <- aov(Jump_cm ~ WarmUp, data = df)
summary(fit)         # fit alone shows no F or p
library(effectsize)
eta_squared(fit, partial = FALSE)
omega_squared(fit)
library(report)
report(fit)

# 3) Pairwise comparisons (Tukey, 95% CIs)
library(emmeans)
pw <- pairs(emmeans(fit, ~ WarmUp), adjust = "tukey")
pw                   # already the table: no summary() needed
confint(pw)          # adds the 95% CIs

# 4) Model diagnostics
library(performance)
check_model(fit)
check_normality(fit)  # helper next to the Q-Q plot
check_homogeneity(fit)  # Bartlett test (Jamovi shows Levene)
check_outliers(fit)  # Cook's distance: influence, not a pass/fail test

# 5) Visualization
boxplot(Jump_cm ~ WarmUp, data = df)
