How to read it 怎麼看
1 · Count the factors. One: the programme, with 3 levels. The jump is the DV, measured once after each programme; it is not a second factor.1 · 數因子。一個：訓練方式，3 個水準。跳躍是依變項（DV），每種訓練之後量一次；它不是第二個因子。
2 · Count the people. 8, not 24. The 24 are jumps.2 · 數人數。是 8，不是 24。24 是跳躍次數。
3 · Count the numbers per person. Three: the same player is in every column → within-subjects → one-way RM ANOVA.3 · 數每個人有幾個數字。三個：同一位球員在每一欄都出現 → 受試者內 → 單因子重複量數變異數分析。
Why not the other two? 為什麼不是另外兩個？
Why not one-way ANOVA: one-way ANOVA needs different players in each level (the right-hand sheet, 24 people). Here every player did all three. One-way ANOVA would treat the 24 jumps as 24 different people and throw away what makes this design strong: each player is her own control.為什麼不是單因子變異數分析：單因子變異數分析需要每個水準是不同的球員（右邊那張表，24 個人）。這裡每位球員三種都做。用單因子變異數分析，會把 24 次跳躍當成 24 個不同的人，丟掉這個設計最強的地方：每位球員自己當對照。
Why not mixed ANOVA: a mixed design needs two factors, one between (different people in different groups) and one within (the same people measured more than once), like the Week 8 study: Group × Time. Here there is one factor, the programme, and nobody was put into a group. “One jump after each” is not a time factor: there is no before and after, only one jump per programme.為什麼不是混合設計變異數分析：混合設計需要兩個因子，一個受試者間（不同的人在不同組）、一個受試者內（同一批人量不只一次），像第八週的研究：組別 × 時間。這裡只有一個因子，訓練方式，而且沒有人被分到任何一組。「每種之後跳一次」不是時間因子：沒有前測和後測，每種訓練只跳一次。

Summary 總結

How to read 怎麼讀
Group: F(1, 13) = 10.76 → the adjusted group difference is 10.76 times the noise. 13 = 16 − 2 groups − 1 covariate.組別：校正後的組間差距是雜訊的 10.76 倍。13 = 16 − 2 組 − 1 個共變數。
p = .006 → if the groups were equal at the same pre-test, a gap at least this large has probability .006. Reject H₀.如果前測相同時兩組相等，至少這麼大的差距出現的機率是 .006。拒絕 H₀。
ηp² = .45 → Group explains 45% of what is left after the pre-test.扣掉前測之後，組別解釋了剩下變異的 45%。
Adjusted means 40.99 vs 39.01 → at a pre-test of 37.50, plyometric adds 1.98 cm, 95% CI [0.68, 3.28].前測 37.50 時，增強式多 1.98 公分，95% CI [0.68, 3.28]。
CMJ_Pre: F = 148.98 → the covariate works: the pre-test predicts the post-test strongly (slope 0.80). Not the research question.共變數有用：前測強烈預測後測（斜率 0.80）。這不是研究問題。
Before you trust it → parallel lines (homogeneity of slopes, F(1, 12) = 0.00, p = .958); residual shape (Shapiro–Wilk W = 0.95, p = .503); and say how the groups were formed.相信它之前 → 兩條線平行（斜率同質性，F(1, 12) = 0.00，p = .958）；殘差形狀（Shapiro–Wilk W = 0.95，p = .503）；還要寫明兩組是怎麼形成的。
APA

An ANCOVA on post-training jump height, with pre-training jump height as the covariate, showed a significant effect of Group, F(1, 13) = 10.76, p = .006, ηp² = .45. Adjusted for the pre-test (M = 37.50 cm), the plyometric group jumped higher than the control group (adjusted M = 40.99 vs 39.01 cm, difference = 1.98 cm, 95% CI [0.68, 3.28]). The regression slopes were homogeneous, F(1, 12) = 0.00, p = .958. Groups were self-selected, not randomised.

MANOVA | Two outcomes

多變量變異數分析｜兩個結果
Summary 總結
  1. Back to the randomised study (Week 8). The coach also timed a 20-m sprint before and after. Two DVs: jump change and sprint change.回到隨機分組的研究（第八週）。教練前後也測了 20 公尺衝刺。兩個依變項：跳躍變化和衝刺變化。
  2. Two separate tests at .05 = two chances of a false positive. Either correct the p values (Holm) or ask one question about the pair: MANOVA.兩個各 .05 的檢定 = 兩次假陽性的機會。要麼校正 p 值（Holm），要麼用 MANOVA 對這一對問一次。
  3. MANOVA asks: do the groups differ on the set of DVs? One statistic (Pillai’s trace), one p. Then look at each DV.MANOVA 問：各組在這一組依變項上有沒有差異？一個統計量（Pillai 跡）、一個 p。然後再看每個依變項。
  4. Use it when the DVs belong together and are correlated (r = −.85 here; two DVs that are almost the same measure need only one). When each DV is its own question, separate tests with a correction are clearer. In 74 department theses: 1 MANOVA.依變項屬於同一件事、彼此相關時（這裡 r = −.85；兩個幾乎相同的量測只需要一個）才用。每個依變項各是一個問題時，分開檢定加校正更清楚。本系 74 篇論文：1 篇 MANOVA。

How to read 怎麼讀
Pillai’s trace = .55 → the groups explain about 55% of the pair of changes taken together. Between 0 and 1 when the factor has two levels.兩個變化量合起來看，各組解釋了約 55%。因子只有兩個水準時介於 0 到 1。

Linear mixed model (LMM) | Missing values

線性混合模型｜缺值
Summary 總結
  1. Three players have one missing test. A mixed ANOVA needs every player at every time: it drops all three (13 left).三位球員各缺一次測驗。混合設計需要每位球員每個時間點都有：三位全被排除（剩 13 位）。
  2. A linear mixed model works on one row per measurement, with the player as a random effect: 29 of 32 measurements, all 16 players.線性混合模型用一列一筆測量的資料，球員是隨機效果：32 筆中的 29 筆，16 位球員全留。
  3. Same question, same line: Group × Time. With nothing missing it gives exactly the Week 8 numbers, F(1, 14) = 9.33, p = .009.同一個問題、同一列：組別 × 時間。沒有缺值時它給的就是第八週的數字，F(1, 14) = 9.33，p = .009。
  4. Trials: when each player jumps three times, rows are trials, not players. Tell the model which rows share a player and a test day; otherwise the trials are treated as independent measurements of the training effect. 16 players × 2 tests × 3 jumps = 96 rows; 5 jumps are missing, so 91 trials are recorded.試作：每位球員跳三次時，列是試作，不是球員。要告訴模型哪些列屬於同一位球員、同一個測驗日；否則這些試作會被當成訓練效果的獨立證據。16 位球員 × 2 次測驗 × 3 跳 = 96 列；缺 5 跳，所以記錄到 91 次試作。
