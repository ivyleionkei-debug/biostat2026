差異是真的嗎？Week 4 · is the difference real?

Assume H₀ (no difference) first, then ask: how far is the observed result from what H₀ predicts? If a result this far would rarely happen when H₀ is true, reject H₀.

先假設 H₀（沒有差別），再問：觀察到的結果離 H₀ 的預期有多遠？如果 H₀ 成立時，這麼遠的結果很少出現，就拒絕 H₀。

stage 0 · the framework

Hypothesis testing: the framework

假設檢定：框架

Hypothesis testing is a decision-making framework built on probability. Its job: turn a research question into a statistical form that data can test.

假設檢定是用機率建立的決策框架。它的功能：把研究問題轉化成可以用資料檢驗的統計形式。

H₀ (null hypothesis): no effect / no difference. Example: the exercise group and the control group have the same mean heart rate.

H₀（虛無假設）：無效果／無差異。例子：運動組與控制組的平均心率沒有差異。

H₁ (alternative hypothesis): an effect / a difference exists. Example: the mean heart rate of the exercise group differs from the control group.

H₁（對立假設）：有效果／有差異。例子：運動組的平均心率與控制組不同。

The logic is refutation: we do not test H₁ directly. We assume H₀ is true, look at the data, and judge whether they are common or rare under H₀.

邏輯是反證法：我們不直接檢驗 H₁，而是先假設 H₀ 為真，看數據，再判斷數據在 H₀ 下常見還是罕見。

Common under H₀ → no reason to reject H₀. Rare under H₀ → reject H₀ and lean towards H₁.

在 H₀ 下常見 → 沒有理由拒絕 H₀；在 H₀ 下罕見 → 拒絕 H₀，傾向 H₁。

H₀ Practice length has no effect: no difference between groups.

stage 1 · paired t-test

H₀, H₁ and the p-value: the paired t-test

H₀、H₁ 與 p 值：配對 t 檢定

Research question: did the heart rate of these 6 people differ before and after burpees?

研究問題：6 個人做 burpee 之前和之後，心率有沒有差別？

What we want to show is H₁: burpees change heart rate. But H₁ does not say by how much, so no probability can be computed from it.

我們想證明的是 H₁：burpee 會改變心率。但 H₁ 沒有說差多少，所以沒辦法直接計算機率。

So we first assume H₀: heart rate before = heart rate after, i.e. the mean difference = 0. H₀ gives one exact number, so we can ask: if H₀ were true, how rare would these data be?

所以先假設 H₀：burpee 前的心率 = burpee 後的心率，也就是差值的平均 = 0。H₀ 給出一個確定的數字，我們才能計算：「如果 H₀ 成立，看到這樣的資料有多罕見？」

Goal: p · 目標：p
Statistic 統計量how far from H₀ 離 H₀ 多遠t = mean diff ÷ SE
df 自由度pairs − 1 = 6 − 1 = 5

statistic + df + reference distribution → p → decision統計量 + 自由度 + 參考分佈 → p → 決定

Differences (after − before): 90, 60, 100, 50, 40, 50 → mean diff = 65.00, SD = 24.29. The software does the arithmetic:

H₀ · null hypothesis 虛無假設

The “no difference” statement. It gives one exact value (difference = 0), so a probability can be computed.

「沒有差別」的假設，給出一個確定的數值（差 = 0），讓我們可以計算機率。

H₁ · alternative hypothesis 對立假設

The claim the researcher wants to support: there is a difference (higher or lower: two-sided).

研究者想支持的說法（有差別；高或低都算，即雙尾）。

t

How many SEs the observed difference is from H₀. t = difference ÷ SE.

觀察到的差距離 H₀ 有幾個 SE。t = 差距 ÷ SE。

p-value p 值

If H₀ were true, the probability of a result at least this extreme. Small p → reject H₀. Statistics cannot prove H₀ false; it only shows the data are rare under H₀.

假設 H₀ 成立，得到至少這麼極端結果的機率。p 很小 → 拒絕 H₀。統計無法「證明」H₀ 是錯的，只能說資料在 H₀ 下很罕見。

Paired samples 配對樣本

Every value is matched with another value from the same person. df = number of pairs − 1.

每個數值都對應到同一個人的另一個數值；自由度 = 配對數 − 1。

  • p = .001 if H₀ were true, a result at least this extreme would occur with probability .001 (both tails of the t distribution, df = 5).如果 H₀ 成立，得到至少這麼極端結果的機率是 .001（自由度 5 的 t 分配，兩邊尾巴）。

Independent t-test: two groups of different people

獨立樣本 t 檢定：兩組不同的人

Different people, so each group has its own SE. Variances add, SEs do not: the two means wobble independently and rarely reach their extremes in the same direction.

不同的人，所以兩組各有自己的 SE。可以相加的是變異數，不是 SE：兩個平均數各自獨立地晃動，很少同時晃到最極端、又同一個方向。

SE_I = SD_I ÷ √n_I = 28.11 ÷ √6 = 11.47
SE_C = SD_C ÷ √n_C = 5.48 ÷ √5 = 2.45
SE_diff = √(SE_I² + SE_C²) = √(11.47² + 2.45²) = 11.73
t = difference ÷ SE_diff = 69.00 ÷ 11.73 = 5.88
95% CI = difference ± t* × SE_diff = 69.00 ± t* × 11.73 = [39.58, 98.42]

Standard error (SE) 標準誤

How much a mean, or a difference between two means, would move if the study were repeated.

研究重做時，平均數（或兩組平均數之差）會晃動多少。

Welch’s t-test Welch t 檢定

Does not assume the two SDs are equal (28.11 vs 5.48 here). The default for a thesis. In Jamovi’s Independent Samples T-Test, “Student’s” is the version that assumes equal SDs.

不假設兩組 SD 相同（這裡是 28.11 對 5.48），論文預設用它。在 Jamovi 的 Independent Samples T-Test，「Student’s」是假設兩組 SD 相同的版本。

Not significant ≠ equal 不顯著 ≠ 相等

p > .05 only says the data are compatible with “no difference”. Read the CI to see what else they are compatible with.

p > .05 只代表資料和「沒有差別」相容，要看 CI 容許的範圍。

stage 3 · chi-square test

Chi-square test: the dependent variable is yes or no

卡方檢定：依變項是「有／沒有」

The DV is re-sprain yes or no: there is no mean, so we compare numbers of people. (Hypothetical data: 40 taped, 40 not taped, one season.)

依變項（DV）是「有／沒有再扭傷」，沒有平均數，只能比較人數。（假設資料：40 人有貼紮、40 人沒有，一個球季。）

H₀：貼紮沒有作用，也就是兩組的再扭傷比例一樣。H₀ 不是說一半的人再扭傷。
