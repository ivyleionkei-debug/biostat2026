APA
A linear mixed model with group, time and their interaction as fixed effects and a random intercept per player (16 players, 29 measurements) showed a group × time interaction, F(1, 11.49) = 8.37statistic · df, p = .014exact p: the plyometric group improved 3.22 cm moreeffect size than the control group, 95% CI [0.92, 5.52]CI.

Effect size = 3.22 cm: the difference in the units of the outcome. A mixed model has no standard η²p, so report the estimate with its 95% CI (Appelbaum et al., 2018; Wilkinson & Task Force on Statistical Inference, 1999). 效果量 = 3.22 公分：用結果變項單位表示的差距。混合模型沒有標準的 η²p，所以報告估計值和 95% CI。
