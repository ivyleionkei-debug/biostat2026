01

Effect size效果量

p alone is not enough. p says whether there is an effect; it does not say how large. And the size of p cannot tell you the size of the effect.只看 p 不夠。p 說的是有沒有效果，沒有說有多大。而且 p 的大小，也推不出效果的大小。

The study 研究

Research question 研究問題Does 8 weeks of plyometric training improve countermovement jump height more than usual training?8 週增強式訓練，是否比一般訓練更能提高下蹲跳高度？

Design 設計16 players · plyometric or control (8 each) · jump height before and after (Week 8 data)16 位球員・增強式組或對照組（各 8 人）・前後各測一次跳躍高度（第 8 週的資料）
依變項（DV）Change in jump height (cm) = Post − Pre跳躍高度的變化（公分）= 後測 − 前測
Result 結果Plyometric +4.00 cm · control +1.00 cm · difference 3.00 cm增強式組 +4.00 公分・對照組 +1.00 公分・差 3.00 公分
p and effect size p 與效果量

Effect size: how large the difference or the relationship is. Cohen’s d: the difference between two means, in SD units. SD (standard deviation): how far the players are from their group mean.效果量：差異或關係有多大。Cohen’s d：兩個平均數的差，以標準差（SD）為單位。標準差（SD）：球員離該組平均數有多遠。

One dot = one player. Vertical line: the group mean. The SD stays 1.96 cm.一點 = 一位球員。直線：該組平均數。標準差固定為 1.96 公分。

A small p can come from a large effect, or only from many people. So after every p, report the effect size.p 小，可以是因為效果大，也可以只是因為人多。所以每個 p 後面都要報告效果量。

02

Confidence interval信賴區間

The size alone is not enough either. The 95% CI says how sure we are about the size.只看大小也不夠。95% 信賴區間說的是我們對這個大小有多確定。

Difference and its 95% CI 差距和它的 95% 信賴區間

95% CI: the range of differences that the data fit. The same study as in 01: difference 3.00 cm.95% 信賴區間：和這份資料相容的差距範圍。和 01 同一個研究：差 3.00 公分。

Dot: the difference, 3.00 cm. Bar: its 95% CI. Dashed line: 0 = no difference.點：差距 3.00 公分。橫條：它的 95% 信賴區間。虛線：0 = 沒有差異。

With few people the interval is wide. Not significant means the interval includes 0. It does not mean the effect is 0.人數少，區間就寬。不顯著代表區間包含 0，不代表效果是 0。

Three numbers, three questions. One number cannot answer the other two.三個數字，三個問題。一個數字回答不了另外兩個。
