兩個數值一起變嗎？Week 6 · do they go together?

Two numbers measured on the same people, no IV: look at the scatterplot first, then ask how closely they go together (r) and how much one changes with the other (b).

同一群人身上量到兩個數值、沒有自變項：先看散布圖，再問兩者一起變得多緊密（r），以及一個變多少時另一個跟著變多少（b）。

stage 0 · same framework, new question

Difference or relationship?

差異，還是關係？

Week 4 ended with one sentence: no IV, only two numbers measured on the same people → a relationship, Week 6.

第四週最後一句：沒有自變項，只有同一群人身上量到的兩個數值 → 那是關係，第六週。

“The same people, two numbers” can be two different questions.

「同一群人、兩個數值」可能是兩種不同的問題。

The same variable measured twice (heart rate at baseline and after set 4): is there a difference? → paired t-test (Week 4).

同一個變項量兩次（基線和第 4 組後的心率）：有沒有差異？→ 配對 t 檢定（第四週）。

Two different variables, each measured once (12-min run distance and VO₂max): do they go together? → a relationship (today).

兩個不同的變項，各量一次（12 分鐘跑距離和 VO₂max）：兩者會不會一起變？→ 關係（今天）。

In a relationship study neither number is the IV or the DV: both are simply measured. r shows that they go together, not which one causes the other.

關係研究裡，兩個數值都不是自變項或依變項：兩個都只是量出來的。r 顯示兩者一起變，不顯示誰造成誰。

A third thing can drive both: runners who train more cover more distance and also have a higher VO₂max. Whether you may speak of a cause comes from the design (random assignment, as in the Week 4 golf study), not from r or p.

可能有第三個因素同時影響兩者：訓練量大的跑者跑得比較遠，VO₂max 也比較高。能不能說「造成」，取決於研究設計（例如第四週高爾夫研究的隨機分派），不是取決於 r 或 p。

The framework does not change: assume H₀, compute a statistic, read p. Only the question and the tool change.

框架不變：先假設 H₀，算出統計量，讀 p。改變的只是問題和工具。

Relationship 關係

Two variables measured on the same people move together: when one is higher, the other tends to be higher (or lower).

同一群人身上的兩個變項一起變動：一個比較高時，另一個傾向比較高（或比較低）。

ρ (rho) and r

ρ is the correlation in the population; r is the one we compute from our sample. H₀ is about ρ.

ρ 是母體的相關；r 是從樣本算出來的相關。H₀ 講的是 ρ。

Correlation ≠ causation 相關 ≠ 因果

Two measured numbers can go together because one drives the other, because a third thing drives both, or by chance in a small sample. The statistic cannot tell these apart; the study design can.

兩個量出來的數值一起變，可能是一個影響另一個、第三個因素同時影響兩者，或小樣本的巧合。統計量分不出這三種情況，研究設計才可以。

stage 1 · correlation

Pearson’s r: do they go together?

皮爾森相關 r：兩者一起變嗎？

Look first: the scatterplot. One dot = one runner: distance on the x-axis, VO₂max on the y-axis.

先看散布圖。一個點 = 一位跑者：x 軸是距離，y 軸是 VO₂max。

r tells two things: the direction (+ rising, − falling) and how closely the dots lie along a straight line. It runs from −1 to +1; 0 means no straight-line relationship.

r 說明兩件事：方向（+ 上升、− 下降），以及點有多貼近一條直線。範圍 −1 到 +1；0 代表沒有直線關係。

Goal: r and its p · 目標：r 和它的 p
Statistic 統計量how far from H₀ 離 H₀ 多遠t = r × √(n − 2) ÷ √(1 − r²)
df 自由度n − 2 = 12 − 2 = 10
Reference distribution 參考分配t distribution, both tailst 分配，兩邊尾巴

statistic + df + reference distribution → p → decision統計量 + 自由度 + 參考分配 → p → 決定

The software computes r from all 12 dots, and its t:

軟體用全部 12 個點算出 r，以及它的 t：

t = r × √(n − 2) ÷ √(1 − r²) = 0.72 × √10 ÷ √(1 − 0.72²) = 3.28

Scatterplot 散布圖

One dot per person, one variable on each axis. Always look at it before you read r.

每人一個點，兩個軸各放一個變項。讀 r 之前一定要先看。

Pearson’s r 皮爾森相關係數

Direction and closeness of a straight-line relationship, −1 to +1. r is itself an effect size; a rough guide: .10 small, .30 medium, .50 large.

直線關係的方向與緊密程度，−1 到 +1。r 本身就是效果量；粗略標準：.10 小、.30 中、.50 大。

Why df = n − 2 為什麼自由度是 n − 2

Testing whether the population Pearson correlation is zero is equivalent to testing whether the slope in simple linear regression is zero. To fit that line, we estimate two parameters from the data: the intercept, which tells us where the line sits, and the slope, which tells us how steep it is. Estimating those two parameters leaves n − 2 residual degrees of freedom. With our twelve runners, that is 12 − 2 = 10.

檢定母體皮爾森相關是否為零，等同於檢定簡單線性迴歸的斜率是否為零。要擬合那條直線，我們必須從資料估計兩個參數：截距決定直線的位置，斜率決定直線的傾斜程度。估計了這兩個參數之後，剩下的殘差自由度就是 n − 2。我們有十二位跑者，所以是 12 − 2 = 10。

  • (10) df = n − 2 = 12 − 2.自由度 = n − 2 = 12 − 2。
  • p = .008 if ρ = 0 were true, an r at least this far from 0 would occur with probability .008 (both tails of t, df = 10).如果 ρ = 0 成立，得到離 0 至少這麼遠的 r，機率是 .008（自由度 10 的 t 分配，兩邊尾巴）。
  • 95% CI [.25, .92] the range of plausible ρ. It is wide because n = 12: the true correlation could be weak or very strong.ρ 的合理範圍。n = 12，所以區間很寬：真正的相關可能很弱，也可能很強。

Look again: one extra runner 再看一次散布圖：多一位跑者

One extreme value can move any result: a mean, a t or an r. Add a runner at 3.10 km with a VO₂max of 38.0: r goes from .72 to .48 (p = .098, n = 13). Move him to 3.25 km and 57.0: r goes up to .79 (p = .001).

一個極端值可以移動任何結果：平均數、t 或 r。加入一位 3.10 km、VO₂max 38.0 的跑者：r 從 .72 變成 .48（p = .098，n = 13）。把他移到 3.25 km、57.0：r 升到 .79（p = .001）。

The rule is the same whichever way p moves: check the measurement. An error → correct it or leave it out, and say so. A real value → keep it and report it (you may add the result without it). Decide from the measurement, not from the p-value.

不論 p 往哪個方向變，規則都一樣：先檢查這筆測量。是錯誤 → 更正或排除，並說明。是真實數值 → 保留並報告（可以另外報告沒有它時的結果）。依測量本身決定，不要依 p 值決定。

Outlier 離群值

A point far from the pattern of the others. With a small n, one outlier can change r a lot.

遠離其他點整體型態的點。n 小的時候，一個離群值就能大幅改變 r。

One dot = one person 一點 = 一人

Every test assumes independent observations. Do not put several measurements of the same person into one analysis as if they came from different people.

每一種檢定都假設觀察值互相獨立。不要把同一個人的多次測量，當成不同的人放進同一個分析。

stage 2 · simple linear regression

Regression: how much, and a prediction

迴歸：變多少，以及預測

Same data, new question: for each extra kilometre, how much higher is VO₂max? And what VO₂max do we predict for a runner who covers 2.80 km?

同一份資料，新的問題：每多跑 1 公里，VO₂max 高多少？跑 2.80 km 的跑者，我們預測他的 VO₂max 是多少？

Correlation is symmetric: swap the two variables and r stays .72. Regression is not: you choose Y, the outcome you want to know, and X, the predictor you already have. The research question makes that choice, not the data.

相關是對稱的：兩個變項對調，r 還是 .72。迴歸不是：你要選 Y（你想知道的結果）和 X（你已經有的預測變項）。這個選擇由研究問題決定，不是由資料決定。

Here: the lab test needs a metabolic cart, a technician and an hour per runner; the 12-minute run needs a track and a stopwatch, so every runner in a study can do it. We know the distance and want the VO₂max it points to: Y (outcome) = VO₂max, X (predictor) = distance.

這裡：實驗室測驗需要氣體分析儀、技術人員，每位跑者一小時；12 分鐘跑只需要跑道和碼錶，研究裡每位跑者都能做。我們知道距離，想知道它指向的 VO₂max：Y（結果）= VO₂max，X（預測變項）= 距離。

The line: ŷ = a + b × x. b, the slope, is the change in Y for one unit of X. a, the intercept, is where the line meets x = 0.

直線：ŷ = a + b × x。b 是斜率：X 增加一個單位時，Y 的變化。a 是截距：直線在 x = 0 的位置。

Swap the roles and the line changes. Predicting distance from VO₂max fits its own line, x̂ = 0.69 + 0.043 × y, by minimising the horizontal gaps instead of the vertical ones. Press “Both” on the right: two different lines, one r.

角色對調，直線就不一樣。用 VO₂max 預測距離時，會另外擬合一條直線 x̂ = 0.69 + 0.043 × y，縮小的是水平距離，不是垂直距離。按右邊的「兩條」：兩條不同的直線，同一個 r。

Can one tool replace another? 一種工具可以取代另一種嗎？

The same question in biomechanics. An IMU and a force plate measure the same ten jumps: r(8) = .99, yet the IMU reads 3.06 cm higher on average. r says the two move together, not that they give the same value.

生物力學也有同樣的問題。IMU 和測力板量同樣 10 次跳躍：r(8) = .99，但 IMU 平均高 3.06 cm。r 只說兩者一起變，不代表數值相同。

Agreement between two tools (validity or reliability) is a different question. Correlation tells us how the two measurements vary together, not how far apart their values are. Bland–Altman analysis and an appropriate ICC can help assess agreement, but interchangeability must be judged against an acceptable error range for the intended use.

兩種工具之間的一致性（效度或信度）是另一個問題。相關告訴我們兩種測量如何一起變動，不是它們的數值相差多少。Bland–Altman 分析和合適的 ICC 可以協助評估一致性，但能否互換，必須依照預定用途可接受的誤差範圍來判斷。
