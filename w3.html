stage 2 · tool 1 工具一

Tool 1 — Probability & p-value

If μ₀ were true, how unusual is our sample?
工具一：機率與 p 值
如果 μ₀ 真的成立，我們的樣本有多極端？

Probability gives us a mathematical language for describing sampling uncertainty.

機率讓我們可以用數學語言描述抽樣的不確定性。

Different random samples give slightly different sample means. How much the sample mean typically varies is described by the standard error.

不同的隨機樣本會得到稍微不同的樣本平均數。樣本平均數通常會晃動多少，可以用標準誤描述。

SE = SD / √n SD = spread of individual observations　個體數值有多分散 SE = spread of sample means　樣本平均數有多分散

“SD describes how spread out individual people or observations are.” “SE describes how much the sample mean would vary across repeated samples.”

「SD 描述個體數值之間有多分散。」「SE 描述如果重複抽樣，樣本平均數會晃動多少。」

Larger n → smaller SE → a more stable sample mean. Changing n does not change SD.

n 越大 → SE 越小 → 樣本平均數越穩定。改變 n 不會改變 SD。

p-value　p 值

Counts in the simulation and area under the model are two ways of representing the same probability.

p-value ≠ probability that H₀ is true　　p 值 ≠ H₀ 為真的機率

The p-value is computed on the sampling distribution of x̄ (width SE), never on the individual-value curve (width SD).
p 值是在樣本平均數的抽樣分布（寬度 SE）上計算，不是在個別數值的分布（寬度 SD）上計算。

A p-value tests one hypothesised population value μ₀. Instead of testing one value at a time, can we estimate a range of population values compatible with our sample?

p 值一次檢驗一個假設的母體值 μ₀。我們可不可以不只檢驗一個值，而是估計一整個與資料相容的母體範圍？

stage 3 · tool 2 工具二

Tool 2 — Confidence interval

Which population values are compatible with our sample?
工具二：信賴區間
哪些母體數值與我們的樣本相容？

A · One sample → one CI　一次抽樣 → 一條信賴區間

Press Step in the first panel. Each press draws one sample and computes its x̄, its SE and its 95% CI.

在第一個面板按「Step」。每按一次：抽一個樣本，算出它的 x̄、SE 和 95% 信賴區間。

95% CI = x̄ ± t* × SE SE = SD / √n

The CI endpoints are not the minimum and maximum observations. One sample → one x̄ → one confidence interval.

CI 的兩端不是樣本中的最小值與最大值。一次抽樣 → 一個 x̄ → 一條信賴區間。

B · CI and p-value are the same uncertainty　CI 與 p 值是同一個不確定性

Second panel: a fixed reference value μ₀ = 50 (a national norm) and a team mean x̄ = 52, SD = 6. Move n and watch the interval and the verdict.

第二個面板：固定參考值 μ₀ = 50（全國常模），球隊平均 x̄ = 52，SD = 6。拉動 n，看區間和結論怎麼變。

P-value and confidence interval are two views of the same sampling uncertainty.

p 值與信賴區間，是同一個抽樣不確定性的兩種看法。

95% CI includes μ₀→ two-sided p ≥ .05 → not statistically significant　區間包含 μ₀ → p ≥ .05 → 不顯著
95% CI excludes μ₀→ two-sided p < .05 → statistically significant　區間不含 μ₀ → p < .05 → 顯著

Instructor note: this equivalence assumes the CI and the test use the same model, standard error and significance level.
教師註：這個對應關係假設 CI 與檢定用同一個模型、同一個標準誤、同一個顯著水準。

C · What does “95%” mean?　「95%」是什麼意思？

Back in the first panel, press Run 100 studies. This is a simulation, so the true μ is deliberately known and drawn as a line.

回到第一個面板按「跑 100 次」。這是模擬，所以真正的 μ 是刻意已知的，畫成一條線。

If we repeated the same sampling procedure many times and calculated a 95% CI each time, about 95% of those intervals would cover the true μ.

如果用同一個方法重複抽樣很多次，而且每次都計一條 95% CI，理論上大約 95% 的區間會覆蓋真正的 μ。

100 studies → 100 sample means → 100 different confidence intervals. They are not averaged into one final CI; “mean width” is the average width of the 100 intervals, not a new interval.

100 次研究 → 100 個樣本平均數 → 100 條不同的信賴區間。它們不會合併成一條新的 CI；「平均寬度」是這 100 條的平均寬度，不是一條新的區間。

95% does NOT mean “a 95% probability that μ is inside this realised interval”. The 95% describes the long-run performance of the method.
95% 不是「μ 有 95% 機率在這一條區間裡」。95% 描述的是這個方法長期的表現。

stage 4 · a ruler 一把尺

The normal model

68–95–99.7: SD as a ruler
常態模型
68–95–99.7：用 SD 當一把尺

If a variable is approximately normally distributed, SD becomes a ruler.

如果一個變項大致符合常態分配，SD 就可以當成一把尺。

Mean ± 1 SD　≈ 68%
Mean ± 2 SD　≈ 95%
Mean ± 3 SD　≈ 99.7% 大約 68% 的數值在平均數 ±1 SD 內；大約 95% 在 ±2 SD 內；大約 99.7% 在 ±3 SD 內。

This gives a very quick way to judge whether the values implied by mean ± SD are plausible.

所以，只看 mean 和 SD，我們已經可以快速檢查這組數據所暗示的範圍是否合理。

Body fat = 18.5 ± 9.2 %　體脂率

Press Body fat on the right. The table computes the ruler: 18.5 − 9.2 = 9.3 %; 18.5 − 18.4 = 0.1 %; 18.5 − 27.6 = −9.1 % — physiologically impossible.

按右邊的「Body fat 體脂」。表格算出這把尺：18.5 − 9.2 = 9.3%；18.5 − 18.4 = 0.1%；18.5 − 27.6 = −9.1%——生理上不可能。

If this variable really followed a symmetric normal model, does this implied distribution make sense?

如果這個變項真的符合左右對稱的常態模型，它所暗示的分配合理嗎？

This is a warning that a symmetric normal model may not describe the variable well. Inspect the distribution: is it skewed? is there a floor? are different groups mixed together? If it is strongly skewed, mean ± SD may not be the most informative summary — consider median + IQR.

這是在警告：左右對稱的常態模型可能不適合這個變項。檢查分配：有沒有偏態？有沒有下限？有沒有把不同群體混在一起？如果明顯偏態，mean ± SD 可能不是最有資訊量的摘要——可以考慮 median + IQR。

An impossible implied value does not by itself prove skewness; it tells you to look.
暗示出不可能的數值本身不能證明偏態；它是叫你去檢查。

stage 5 · the theorem 定理

Central Limit Theorem

Why can sample means become normal even when raw data are not?
中央極限定理
為什麼原始資料不是常態，平均數仍可以接近常態？

Raw observations may be strongly skewed. Now imagine repeatedly taking samples from this population.

原始資料可以非常偏態。現在想像我們不斷從這個母體抽樣。

1Each sample has 1 person → the distribution looks like the raw data　每個樣本只有 1 個人 → 看到的就是原始資料
2Each sample has 2 people → take the mean of those 2　每個樣本 2 個人 → 取這 2 人的平均
3Each sample has 3 people → take the mean of those 3　每個樣本 3 個人 → 取 3 人的平均
5Each sample has 5 people → take the mean of those 5　每個樣本 5 個人 → 取 5 人的平均

As the number of observations in each sample increases:

當每個樣本包含的人愈多：

1. the centre stays near the population mean
2. the distribution of sample means becomes narrower
3. the distribution of sample means becomes more symmetric and more normal-shaped

1. 樣本平均數的中心仍然接近母體平均數
2. 樣本平均數的分布愈來愈窄
3. 樣本平均數的分布愈來愈對稱、愈接近鐘形

As n within each sample increases, the sampling distribution of x̄ becomes narrower and more approximately normal. This is the Central Limit Theorem.

當每一個樣本內的 n 增加，樣本平均數 x̄ 的抽樣分配會變窄，而且愈來愈接近常態。這就是中央極限定理。

THE RAW DATA DID NOT BECOME NORMAL.
THE DISTRIBUTION OF SAMPLE MEANS BECAME MORE NORMAL.

不是原始資料變成常態。
是樣本平均數的抽樣分配愈來愈接近常態。

“n = 5” means five people INSIDE each sample — not repeating the study five times.
「n = 5」是指每個樣本裡面有五個人，不是把研究重複做五次。

Once the sampling distribution becomes regular, we can ask what proportion of possible sample means fall above a threshold — probability as a proportion, as in Stage 2.
當抽樣分布變得規則之後，我們可以問：有多大比例的樣本平均數會落在某個門檻以上——這又是把機率看成比例，和 Stage 2 一樣。
